# Contributing to Tyllage

> **Tyllage does not accept outside contributions.**
> All Tyllage repositories are proprietary to Tyllage LLP and are maintained only by the Tyllage team. Pull requests, patches and feature work from anyone outside the team are closed without review. Forking or copying a Tyllage repository does not give you any right to use, run, modify or redistribute its code. See the repository's `LICENSE`.

If you are not a member of the Tyllage team:

- **Found a security problem?** Report it privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Interested in licensing or a partnership?** Email [tyllage.co@gmail.com](mailto:tyllage.co@gmail.com).

## For Tyllage team members

- Each repository has its own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with setup steps and conventions. Follow it where it exists.
- Work from a branch off the default branch (for example `feat/...`, `fix/...` or `docs/...`) and merge through a pull request.
- All work you do on Tyllage repositories belongs to Tyllage LLP.
- Do not copy Tyllage code, data or documents into personal projects, other repositories or public places (gists, forums, AI tools that keep your data) without approval.
- Never commit secrets, `.env` files or real customer, farm or financial data.
