# Code of Conduct

Tyllage is maintained by the Tyllage team and does not accept outside contributions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). We expect every Tyllage project space to be respectful and safe for team members, partners, customers and anyone who contacts us, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- being respectful and professional;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- protecting confidential, personal and commercially sensitive information.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances;
- publishing others' private information, or partner or customer data, without permission;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Tyllage team members in all Tyllage project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies to anyone who interacts with those spaces, and when someone represents Tyllage elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to [tyllage.co@gmail.com](mailto:tyllage.co@gmail.com) or through GitHub's **Report content** option. Reports are handled in confidence.

Tyllage may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
